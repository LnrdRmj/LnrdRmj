## Projects I'm proud of

**Yoomy** was a platform where students could order their snack at school online. The idea was to bring something like Just Eat into schools. I built it as my thesis project, with Vue and CapacitorJS for the app and Node, Express and Prisma on the backend, and I wrote the tests with Jest. It isn't running anymore, and the repository is private, but you can find it at [App-Food-Delivery/yoomy_app](https://github.com/App-Food-Delivery/yoomy_app).

The other one is a management dashboard for GPS trackers, built for the company that makes them. You pick a time range and see the route a tracker took, along with the data it collected: temperature, speed, pressure, inclination and more.
